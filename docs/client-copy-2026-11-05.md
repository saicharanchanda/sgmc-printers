## Applied

The client's page-by-page copy (home, products, infrastructure, about, contact, footer) was used
as written, including the "Shree Gayathri" spelling. The V5 brief was used for structure and
guard-rails.

- **Home:** all 10 sections in order. The hero highlights replace the "45+" and "100%" counters.
  The process steps are back on the home page. The home quote form is replaced by the client's
  final call to action.
- **Navigation:** Home · About Us · Products · Services · Infrastructure · Contact, plus
  "Request a Quote".
- **Products hub:** the client's six categories, taglines and button labels; the item counts are
  removed. Product and service pages now use "Enquire About This Product" / "Discuss This Service",
  and the WhatsApp messages are pre-filled with the product or service.
- **Services hub:** the brief's hero ("Every Stage of Printing. One Experienced Team.") and the
  client's tagline and description per service.
- **Infrastructure:** the client's copy for each department. Machine cards are text-only until each
  machine has its own photo, so one stock photo no longer stands in for several different machines
  (brief §9).
- **About:** philosophy, experience, people, and leadership with the client's descriptions.
- **Contact:**
  - The form now has Company, plus optional Size, Page count, Colours, Binding and Delivery date
    (folded away under "Add specifications").
  - The submit button reads "Send My Printing Enquiry".
  - The closing message is added.
- **Footer:** the client's brand text, quick links, "Our Commitment" line, copyright and motto.
  The colophon is removed.
- **Removed claims (brief §18):** "Nothing is outsourced", "perfect run", "100% in-house", "45+",
  "without outsourcing", "economical short runs", and the reply-time promises.
- **Captions and alt text:** stock photos are now described as "illustrative image", never as
  SGMC's machines or premises.
